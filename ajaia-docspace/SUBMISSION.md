## Demo flow

1. Open the application and log in with Mahnoor.
2. Create a document, add formatted rich text, and wait for the Saved status.
3. Refresh and reopen the document; confirm formatting persists.
4. Share the document with Alex as viewer.
5. Log out and log in as Alex; confirm the document appears read-only.
6. Log back in as Mahnoor, change Alex to editor, then log in as Alex and edit.
7. Confirm Alex cannot delete the document or manage sharing.
8. Import `.txt` or `.md` content, then demonstrate owner deletion.
9. Log out and confirm protected document APIs require authentication.
